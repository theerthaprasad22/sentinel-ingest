What is real here. Four of the five sources are live public feeds — Remotive, Arbeitnow, We Work Remotely and Hacker News — pulled through their published APIs and RSS. The fifth, sandbox, is a hostile job board served by this same process: its listings are synthetic and labelled as such everywhere they appear. Every number on this page is measured from real traffic; none of it is seeded, mocked or hand-written. No LinkedIn, Indeed or Naukri account was touched, per the brief's scope guardrail.
